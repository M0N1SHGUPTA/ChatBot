### 3.6 Frequently Asked Questions (About FAQ)
**Q1: How is JIBB governed and structured?**
A: JIBB operates as an independent non-profit organization with a dual-headquarters structure in Tokyo and Noida. It is managed by an executive Leadership Team and advised by a distinguished Advisory Board of former government dignitaries, academics, and industrial leaders from both nations.

**Q2: Is JIBB officially affiliated with the Japanese or Indian governments?**
A: JIBB is formally approved by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. While maintaining complete operational autonomy, JIBB collaborates closely with Indian central and state bodies (Invest India, DPIIT, Invest UP, Invest Odisha, GSEM) and Japanese trade associations and SMEs.

**Q3: How are the Advisory Board members selected?**
A: Advisory Board members are invited based on lifetime contributions to bilateral trade, diplomacy, or technological innovation. They serve voluntarily to guide high-level macroeconomic alignment.

**Q4: How are funding and membership revenues utilized?**
A: All revenue from memberships, sponsorships, and consulting services is 100% reinvested into core non-profit programs: sponsoring trade delegations, maintaining physical testing laboratories, conducting primary research, and supporting cross-border startups.

**Q5: What is JIBB's policy on data privacy and corporate confidentiality?**
A: JIBB enforces strict compliance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. All proprietary technology and client discussions are protected under legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

## 7. PAGE: JAPAN-INDIA SEMICONDUCTOR COMMITTEE (JISC)
- **Canonical URL**: `https://npo-jibb.org/en/jisc`
- **Page Title**: Japan-India Semiconductor Committee (JISC) | Building Resilient Chip Supply Chains
- **Core Identity**: JISC is a specialized bilateral committee established under JIBB dedicated exclusively to building resilient semiconductor and high-tech manufacturing ecosystems between Japan and India.

### 7.1 Strategic Mission & Objectives
- **Supply Chain Decentralization**: Mitigating global geopolitical risks by establishing a trusted, geographically diversified manufacturing and supply chain corridor between Japan and India.
- **The Materials & Equipment Backbone**: While chip design and assembly capture headlines, Japan controls over 50% of the world's semiconductor manufacturing equipment and over 60% of critical semiconductor materials (silicon wafers, photoresists, CMP slurries, target metals). JISC connects these Japanese suppliers with Indian infrastructure.
- **Strategic Hubs**:
  - **Noida, Uttar Pradesh (India)**: 6th Floor, A-162, Sector 136, Arihant Business Centre, Noida. Housing display facilities, testing labs, and bilateral meeting rooms.
  - **Tokyo (Japan)**: Tameike Suzuki Building 3F, 1-3-13 Akasaka, Minato-ku, Tokyo.

### 7.2 The Quad-Pillar Collaborative Model
1. **Academic Synergy**: High-risk R&D, experimentation, and advanced semiconductor talent development between Indian and Japanese universities.
2. **Corporate Synergy**: Providing market scale, manufacturing pathways, and commercial distribution channels.
3. **High-Tech Physical Infrastructure**: Specialized wet labs, fabrication tools, testing & verification suites, and technical libraries.
4. **TRL Lifecycle Pipeline**: Structured progression through Technology Readiness Levels:
   - *Nascent Stage (TRL 1–3)*: Foundational research, patent filings, and risk de-risking.
   - *Validation Stage (TRL 4–6)*: Prototype testing, cleanroom stress characterizations, and design verification.
   - *Maturity & Commercialization (TRL 7–9)*: Volume manufacturing, qualification runs, and global market integration.
